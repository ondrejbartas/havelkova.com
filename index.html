About HH

Clothes of evident nobleness and precise elaboration, with a feeling for elegant feminine lines.

Prague-born Hana Havelková is the winner of the Mode Designer Cup in Frankfurt and the designer of the famous white evening dress in which Taťána Kuchařová won Miss World 2006.

She prefers an austere and simple style — each piece in her collections emphasised with a specific, purified detail. She is best known for her evening and wedding dresses.

In 1991 she established her brand and opened her fashion studio in Prague's Old Town.

Atelier & Recognition

A studio with a global stage

Hana Havelková's collections have been shown around the world — at the Paris Luxury Trade Show, a New York Fashion Week event at Platform 2, and exhibitions Imprints of Prague Architecture in Paris, Berlin, Moscow, Vienna and Brussels.

Since 1998 she has created fashion-show presentations for Škoda Auto, held in Frankfurt, Leipzig, Hamburg, Paris and Prague. She has twice won Designer of the Season in the Czech fashion designers' competition Fashion Point.
